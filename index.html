👋 Tips for grown-ups
  • The camera only works when this page is opened from https:// or localhost. Allow camera access when asked.
  • Scan in good, even light. Avoid shiny glare on the stickers. The 🔦 button turns on the flashlight when the phone has one.
  • Keep the cube held the same way up while scanning. The solving pictures use the same hold as the first photo.
  • 4×4 and 5×5 solutions are long (a few hundred turns) because they build the cube piece by piece. Take breaks! Your place is shown on the progress bar.
  • The move code in the corner (like R, U', 2R) is standard cube notation for older kids.
